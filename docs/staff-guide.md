# GymTrace: front desk guide

For gym attendants at Babcock University Fitness Centre. Keep this next to the front-desk computer.

**Sign in:** go to **gymtrace-rose.vercel.app/admin/login** and use **your own** email and password. Never share your account: every payment and check-in records who did it.
**First day only:** open **General Settings** → **Your name** → type your full name → **Save name**.

---

## 1. A member pays (cash or bank transfer)

1. Click **Members**. Type the member's name or email in the search box.
2. Click **Record payment** on their row.
3. Choose the plan: **Monthly Membership (₦25,000)** or **Day Pass (₦500)**.
4. Under **Paid by**, choose **Cash** or **Bank transfer**.
5. Check **Amount received**. It fills in the price for you. Only change it if the manager agreed a different amount, and then write the reason in **Note**.
6. **Bank transfer:** type the transfer reference from the member's bank app or receipt. **Cash:** type the number from the paper receipt book, if you used one.
7. Click **Record ₦…**. You'll see **Payment recorded** and a receipt number (GT-…). The member's plan starts at once.

- A member with an active monthly plan can't buy a day pass; the form says so. Paying monthly again adds 30 more days.
- **Made a mistake?** Don't record another payment to fix it. Write down the receipt number (GT-…) and tell the manager.

## 2. Print a receipt

- Right after recording: click **View receipt**, then **Print / Save PDF**.
- Later: click **Payments**, find the payment, click **Receipt**, then **Print / Save PDF**.
- Members can also see and print their own receipts on their **Payments** page.

## 3. Check a member in with their QR code

1. Click **Check-in Scanner** (**Scan** on a phone). Allow the camera if the browser asks.
2. The member opens GymTrace on their phone and taps **Generate Qr** on their dashboard. Hold the phone in front of the camera.
3. Wait for the result. **Look at the name on screen and make sure it's the person in front of you.**
4. Click **Scan Next Member**.

## 4. Manual check-in (phone dead, no data, camera broken)

1. **Ask for their student or staff ID card first.** Check that the name and photo match the person.
2. In **Manual Check-in**, type their **email address** (the one they registered with) and click **Check In**.
3. Read the result exactly as for a QR scan.

Never check someone in without seeing their ID card. Manual check-in has no QR code to prove who they are.

## 5. What the scanner messages mean

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Access Granted** | Paid up. The visit is recorded. | Let them in. |
| **Already Checked In** (at a time) | This member was already let in within the last 5 minutes. Nothing extra was charged. | Check that the person in front of you is the member named on screen (look at their student ID) before letting them in. If it isn't them, don't admit them. |
| **Invalid or Expired QR Code** | The code is over a minute old (e.g. a screenshot) or isn't a GymTrace code. | Ask them to tap **Generate Qr** again and show the live code. |
| **This QR code has already been used** | That exact code was already scanned. | Ask for the new code on their phone. |
| **Member Not Found** | No account with that email or ID. | Check the spelling. If they never registered, they sign up first. |
| **No active plan. Payment is needed at the front desk.** | They haven't paid. | Take payment (section 1), then scan again. |
| **Monthly membership has expired.** / **Day pass has expired.** | Their plan ran out. | Take a new payment, then scan again. |
| **Today's day pass has already been used.** | A day pass is one entry only. | They need another day pass or a monthly plan. |
| **Account deactivated. Entry refused.** | The gym has blocked this account. | Don't let them in. Refer them to the manager. |
| **This is a trainer account / admin account, not a gym member.** | Staff accounts don't get member entry. | Follow the gym's staff entry rules. |
| **Camera Error** | The browser can't use the camera. | Allow the camera for this site (lock icon in the address bar), or use manual check-in. |
| **Your session has expired. Please sign in again.** | You've been signed out. | Sign in again. |
| **Network error** | No internet connection. | Check the Wi-Fi, then try again. |

## 6. Deactivate or reactivate an account

Use this when someone must not use the gym (banned, left the university, account misused). Nothing is deleted: their payments and visits stay on record.

1. Go to **Members** (or **Trainers** for trainers) and find the person.
2. Click the **person-with-a-cross** icon (**Deactivate**). Write a short reason, then click **Deactivate**.
   They're signed out at once, and they can't sign in or check in. Their plan keeps running.
3. To undo it, click the **person-with-a-tick** icon (**Reactivate**), then **Reactivate**.

Only deactivate when the manager says so.

## 7. Common questions

- **"I didn't get the verification email."** Tell them to check spam/junk. They can click **Resend verification email** on the login page.
- **"I forgot my password."** On the login page they click **Forgot password?** and follow the email. Staff can't see or reset passwords.
- **"My QR page says No Gym Access."** Their plan isn't active. Check **Members** → their plan, and take payment if needed.
- **New trainer signed up?** They can't be booked until a manager clicks **Approve for booking** on the **Trainers** page.

## 8. End of the day

Open **Payments**. The **Today** card shows totals for **Cash**, **Transfer** and **Card (test)**. The cash total should match the money in the cash box. Report any difference to the manager the same day.
